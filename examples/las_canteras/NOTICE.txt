Las Canteras analytical documentation figures

Work derived from PNOA LiDAR 2023 CC BY 4.0 scne.es (IGN and participating
administrations). DTM generated from ground-classified returns, 0.5 m cells,
1 m IDW interpolation radius; no vertical transformation.
Work derived from PNOA maximum-actuality WMS imagery, CC BY 4.0 scne.es.
Imagery acquisition date is not established; it is not labelled as a 2023 image.

Building outlines derive from Direccion General del Catastro INSPIRE Buildings,
municipality 35017, snapshot 2026-08-21. These are transformed analytical figures,
not an official cadastral product or a redistribution of the original dataset.
The separate promenade.zip contains a small selection of cadastral features
and original LiDAR returns. Its README gives the source tiles, selection coordinates and reuse
terms. The full municipal archive, full tiles and DTM are not bundled.
The project MIT licence does not replace source-data terms.

Modifications: selected and labelled building parts A/B; rendered point-class
and elevation plots; histogram; derived ground raster; WMS image reprojected to
EPSG:4083 for the context panel. Source coordinates were not shifted or repaired.
See sources.json for source URLs, acquisition information and figure hashes.

las-canteras-overview.png is a project screenshot of the wider reconstruction.
promenade-extent.png plots the selected cadastral buildings and LiDAR crop in
EPSG:4083. promenade-model.png renders the LoD1 CityJSON produced by building
the downloadable promenade.zip example, using colour to distinguish buildings.
